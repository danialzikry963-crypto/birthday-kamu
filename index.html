My Beloved Lady,

May good things come your way. ❤️

On this beautiful night, let me have the honour of being the first to wish you a day filled with happiness, love, and everything your heart deserves. It has been exactly 410 days since we began this journey together, and somehow, every single day since then has become one of the most precious chapters of my life. Every moment with you has been a memory worth keeping, and every day beside you has been a gift I never want to take for granted.

Thank you for every laugh we’ve shared, every conversation that stayed in my heart, every memory we’ve created, and every little moment that made our time together special. Thank you for teaching me that love can be patient, that love can be sincere, and that being loved by someone can feel like home. For every lesson, every moment, and every piece of happiness I’ll always be grateful for you.

Even through the storms we’ve faced, we chose to stand beside each other and face every challenge together. We never let the hard days become the end of our story; instead, they became the moments that brought us closer and made our love grow stronger than before.

Oh my darling, Please give me your apologies for all my shortcomings my dear. Forgive me for the days I couldn’t be by your side, for the flowers that never reached your hands, and for the luxuries I couldn’t give you. I may not have much to offer, but what I have, I’ll always give with a genuine heart because loving you was never about how much I could buy, but how much of myself I could give.

But one thing I know for sure:

Wherever life takes you, through every high and every low, just know that I’ll always be there a quiet presence you can always come home to.

Please, always be yourself. I love you for exactly who you are your smile, your little flaws, and every little thing that makes you, you. You don’t need to be perfect, because to me, you already are.

To Another Beautiful Year of You My Dear Lady ❤️
